Our Story

Cooked slow.
Served with love.

MS Biriyani began with a simple belief — food cooked with a mother's love tastes different. Every handi is layered with long-grain rice, house-ground masalas and saffron, sealed with dough and slow-cooked on dum until the aroma fills the room.

Today our doors on Imperial Road open to families, friends and celebrations — from quick lunch meals to grand evenings in the MS Party Hall upstairs. The recipes haven't changed. Neither has the love.

The Dum Craft

Four steps. No shortcuts.

01

Marinate

Yoghurt, house masalas and time — overnight, never rushed.

02

Layer

Par-boiled basmati, saffron milk, fried onions and mint, stacked by hand.

03

Seal & dum

The handi is sealed with dough and rested over a slow flame.

04

Rest & serve

Ten minutes of rest, then opened at your table, steaming.
